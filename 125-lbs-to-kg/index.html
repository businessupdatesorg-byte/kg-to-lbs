Step-by-Step Mathematical Calculation

To convert 125 pounds (lbs) into kilograms (kg), we rely on the international standard conversion factor established under the 1959 International Yard and Pound Agreement:

  • 1 pound (lb) is legally defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms (kg).
  • 1 kilogram (kg) is equal to approximately 2.20462262185 pounds (lb).

The Standard Conversion Formula

To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply by 0.45359237: 

mass (kg) = 125 * 0.45359237

Therefore, 125 lbs = 56.699 kg.
